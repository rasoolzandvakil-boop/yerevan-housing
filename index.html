Bottom line

Yerevan is past its policy-fueled peak: the mortgage-subsidy expiry delivered a flat 2025 (the center fell 1–2 percent) and the market has re-accelerated since Q2 2026 on unsubsidized, increasingly non-resident demand. A selective purchase — quality Kentron secondary stock or the strong mid-ring, seismically sound, 50–75 sq m, bought against cadastre values rather than elite asking prices — should earn roughly 8–12 percent dram-terms total return (4.5–6 percent net yield plus 3–7 percent appreciation), with the dollar outcome hostage to the dram in both directions.

The two questions that dominate the expected outcome sit outside the housing data: the exchange rate, and the November 2026 investment-residency decision, which could convert an ordinary property investment into the anchor of a residence strategy. Waiting for that decision text costs little and may be worth a great deal.
