Who Measures This Market

Every organization that produces data, recurring reports, or forecasts on the Armenian housing market. One structural fact frames the list: no international property consultancy currently covers Armenia and no Big-4 firm publishes market research here — the intelligence base is official data, one flagship private report, and a handful of independents.

Core official data — the four measuring instruments

State Cadastre Committee — Market Analysis monthly + annual Armenian only free
The primary transaction dataset: purchase-sale counts, price per sq m by district, rents, mortgage deals, foreign-buyer counts. The PDFs are Armenian-only; most readers get them via ARKA/Finport/b24 republications.

CBA — Residential Property Price Index quarterly EN free
The cleanest price series: hedonic index built with IMF technical assistance, apartments vs houses, Yerevan zones vs regions. URL pattern …/RPPI Report 20XXQX_eng.pdf is stable.

CBA — Financial Stability Report annual EN free
The risk lens: price-to-rent, oversupply warnings, LTV caps, construction-credit exposure. The most cautious institutional voice on this market.

ArmStat monthly EN/AM/RU free
Construction volumes, completions, permits, housing stock — the supply side. The Ministry of Finance macro reviews add a periodic real-estate section.

The flagship private report

Grant Thornton Armenia — Annual Real Estate Market Analysis annual · ~March EN free
The single best private publication: transactions, district trends, foreign-buyer flows, dashboards plus PDF. Latest outlook (Mar 2026): demand sustained through 2026; watch for anticipatory buying before the January 2027 regional subsidy cutoff.

Institutional assessments

IMF Article IV & SBA reviews — valuation estimates, mortgage-subsidy analysis, macro projections.

ADB / World Bank / EBRD — the ADB housing-finance sector assessment (2025) is the sharpest single document ("approaching the peak of its cycle"); World Bank updates track construction; EBRD covers the macro.

Independent analysts and think tanks

tvyal.com — Aghasi Tavadyan weekly EN — the best independent quant voice; housing affordability and price analyses; cautionary on inflow-driven growth.

Orbeli Analytical Center — occasional deep dives; sees a long-term upward trend, no bubble.

Amberd Research Center (ASUE) — academic; the go-to on bubble/construction-boom questions (mostly Armenian). Also: Luys Foundation (credit-boom skeptic), HfX Housing Studies Centre (affordability).

EVN Report — best English long-form journalism on the market, 1–3 pieces a year.

Practitioner content, portals, STR analytics

Vardanyan & Partners (armenian-lawyer.com) — deepest English series for foreign buyers: price history, yields, STR rules, purchase mechanics.

SIEVN / Melkonyan Substack — relocant-facing commentary with explicit forecasts (3–7% prime, 2026–27); read as sentiment, not analysis. Roxwell Advisors does bespoke commissioned studies.

korter.am / karucapatoxic.am — new-build asking prices per sq m by district; the best free primary-market proxy. The big classifieds (list.am, myrealty.am, estate.am) publish no analytics.

AirDNA · Airbtics · AirROI — Yerevan STR metrics; levels disagree (definitions), so triangulate. Plus Global Property Guide (stale since 2022) and Numbeo (crowdsourced, thin base).

Monthly signal — English wires

ARKA · Finport (full district tables) · ArmBanks (RPPI, mortgages, credit) · b24.am · Armenpress — how non-Armenian-readers track the monthly official releases.

Skip: commercial report vendors. Statista, 6Wresearch, Allied Market Research, MarkWide and similar sell "Armenia Real Estate Market" reports at $1,500–4,500 that recycle official data into templates — some forecasts date from 2021. Fitch Solutions and EIU have no Armenia real-estate product. Grant Thornton + Cadastre + CBA are strictly better, and free.
